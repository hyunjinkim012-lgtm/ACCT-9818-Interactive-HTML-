Difference = Interest Expense − Cash Coupon. A positive difference increases the bond's book value (discount amortization); a negative difference decreases it (premium amortization). Period 0 shows the initial issue price. The final period is adjusted for rounding to finish at face value.

Valuation: Issue price = PV of coupon payments + PV of face value, discounted using the market interest rate per payment period. Effective interest: beginning book value × market rate per period. Rates shown in the dropdowns are annual nominal rates, divided by payment frequency.
